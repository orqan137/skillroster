# Code of Conduct

[한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · **English**

SkillRoster aims to provide a community where anyone can ask questions and contribute safely.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code and proposals, not people.
- Account for differences in experience and background.
- Support disagreements with evidence and reproducible examples.
- Never post personal data, internal company material, or credentials in a public Issue or Pull Request.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, insults, and repeated disruption are not tolerated.

This policy applies to repository Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, commits, official presentations, and public activity representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates this policy and may warn or restrict a participant. Responses consider severity, repetition, and impact on other participants.

Report conduct concerns privately rather than opening a public Issue. The project currently uses the private contact route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where practical and recuse themselves from decisions involving a conflict of interest.
