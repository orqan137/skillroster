# SkillRoster brand guide

[한국어](BRAND.md) · **English**

The SkillRoster identity represents a practical product: organizing scattered team agent skills and assembling the right versions for a project.

## Name and description

- Product name: `SkillRoster`, with no space and capital `S` and `R`
- English: `A Git-backed team roster for reviewing AI-agent skills and assembling project-specific skill sets.`
- Korean: `팀의 AI 에이전트 스킬을 평가하고 프로젝트에 연결하는 Git 기반 로스터`
- Avoid claims of guaranteed correctness, employee surveillance, an implemented central SaaS, or SSO.

## Logo

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| [`skillroster-mark.svg`](./brand/skillroster-mark.svg) | App icon, favicon, and square profile image |
| [`skillroster-wordmark.svg`](./brand/skillroster-wordmark.svg) | README, presentations, and wide headers |

The left line represents Git history. The three slots represent published skills, and the differently colored tiles represent project-specific selection after team review. Keep clear space of at least 25% of the mark's height and do not render it below 24 px.

Do not stretch the logo, change its proportions, add shadows, gradients, or outlines. On a dark background, the wordmark text may become white; keep the colors inside the mark unchanged.

## Rovi mascot

<p align="center">
  <img src="./brand/rovi-mascot.png" width="360" alt="Rovi, the SkillRoster border collie, organizing three skill tiles">
</p>

Rovi is a border collie team coach who sorts skills and helps assemble a project roster. The character is an assistant, not a manager who monitors or controls people.

- Personality: intelligent, calm, and collaborative
- Typical actions: sorting skill tiles, assembling a project lineup, checking change history
- Avoid: aggressive guard-dog imagery, herding people, or exaggerated children's-character expressions
- Name: an original name derived from `Ro` in Roster and a short directional sound
